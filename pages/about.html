ABOUT THE FRONT

The European Popular Front (EPF) is a left-wing and European internationalist platform for political discussion, organisation, and collaboration among individuals who share the goals of solidarity, social justice, anti-capitalism, democracy, internationalism and progressive thought.

The EPF is committed to furthering the social and class struggle of the people of Europe, and aims to aid in achieving a unification of the European continent in the name of proletarian internationalism as well as the establishment of leftist governance across Europe. The EPF does not adhere to a single political ideology or tradition, but rather aims to unite the European left to further the goals outlined here in unity.

The EPF explicitly rejects chauvinism, xenophobia, bigotry and exclusionary or reactionary politics in all forms. The EPF is ultimately a project of hope: that another Europe; another World; is possible, and building it requires a united left capable of thinking and acting beyond borders.
