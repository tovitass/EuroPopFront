WHO WE ARE

The European Popular Front (EPF) is a left-wing Eurofederalist project and a political home for those who don’t fit into existing spaces.

We bring together socialists, social democrats, and left federalists to build a democratic, united Europe based on equality and solidarity.

EPF is a popular front. A broad tent of leftist perspectives united by democracy, equality, and a federal Europe.
