WHAT WE STAND FOR

EPF is a left-wing Eurofederalist project that brings together socialists, social democrats, and left federalists under a common banner. We believe Europe's future lies in unity, not fragmentation; in equality, not hierarchy; in democracy, not technocracy.

Core Principles

  • European Unity: We reject nationalism and borders. Europe must unite as a federal democracy.
  • Social Justice: Economic equality, workers' rights, and social welfare for all Europeans.
  • Democratic Socialism: Markets serve people, not the other way around. Public ownership of key industries.
  • Environmental Protection: A Green New Deal for Europe, transitioning to sustainable energy.
  • Anti-Fascism: We stand against all forms of extremism and authoritarianism.

OUR HISTORY

Founded in 2024, EPF emerged from the recognition that traditional European politics failed to address the continent's deepest challenges. As nationalism rose and inequality grew, we saw the need for a new political force that transcends borders and unites the left.

Starting as an online community, EPF has grown into a think tank and political movement, bringing together activists, intellectuals, and organizers from across Europe. Our work focuses on policy development, community building, and strategic organizing for a federal Europe.

WHO WE ARE

EPF is not a single ideology — it's a popular front. We welcome socialists and communists, social democrats and progressives, left federalists and Europeanists, environmental activists, labor organizers, and anyone committed to a united, leftist Europe.

We are not a party, but a movement. Not a sect, but a broad tent. Our strength lies in our diversity and our shared commitment to European unity and social justice.

OUR STRUCTURE

EPF operates with a central command structure that coordinates our activities while maintaining flexibility for local initiatives. We have a Central Committee for strategic direction, Working Groups for issue-based work, Regional Coordinators for local engagement, and online platforms for coordination.
