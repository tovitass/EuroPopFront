ABOUT THE FRONT

The {{orgFullName}} ({{orgShortName}}) is a broad, big-tent pan-European community bringing together democratic socialists, social democrats, eco-socialists, trade unionists, and progressive left currents. We provide a collaborative space to engage across different traditions, cultivate a genuine pan-European political culture, and work toward a common European future.

At its core, {{orgShortName}} exists to deepen European integration through a leftist, Eurofederalist lens. We believe continental challenges (like economic inequality, climate change, and rising authoritarianism) cannot be solved through nationalism. They require trans-border cooperation, democratic institutions acting at scale, and a vision grounded in solidarity rather than austerity.

{{orgShortName}} is explicitly anti-fascist, anti-racist, anti-authoritarian, and anti-exclusionary. We reject chauvinism, xenophobia, and reactionary politics in all forms. Operating as both a think-tank and a strategic forum, we value intellectual openness and rigorous analysis to build realistic alternatives capable of shaping the continent.

{{orgFullName}} is ultimately a project of hope: a belief that another Europe is possible, and that building it requires a left capable of thinking and acting beyond borders.
